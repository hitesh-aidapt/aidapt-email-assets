Step 2

Add it to Outlook

Pick your version of Outlook. Add the signature once, then tell Outlook to use it for new emails and for replies.

Outlook for Mac

New and classic Outlook on a Mac

  1. In the menu bar, choose Outlook then Settings, and click Signatures.
  2. Click +, name it Aidapt, click into the editor and press Cmd + V.
  3. Under Choose default signature, pick your account and set both New messages and Replies/forwards to Aidapt.
  4. Close the window. Outlook saves as you go.

New Outlook and the web

New Outlook for Windows and outlook.office.com

  1. Click the Settings gear at the top right.
  2. Open Accounts then Signatures.
  3. Click New signature, name it Aidapt, paste with Ctrl + V and click Save.
  4. Under Select default signatures, set both new messages and replies and forwards to Aidapt. Click Save.

Classic Outlook

The Windows desktop app with a File menu

  1. Go to File, Options, Mail, then Signatures.
  2. Click New, name it Aidapt, click into Edit signature and press Ctrl + V.
  3. On the right, set both New messages and Replies/forwards to Aidapt, then click OK.

Step 3

Set your Teams background

One background with your name and title top left, the logo bottom right, and the centre clear for you.

Microsoft Teams

Desktop app on Mac or Windows

  1. Download a background above.
  2. Before you join, click Effects and avatars under your video preview. In a call, use More, then Video effects and avatars.
  3. Under backgrounds, choose Add new and pick the file.
  4. Select it and click Apply. Teams remembers it for your next calls.

Zoom

For calls clients host on Zoom

  1. Open Settings, then Background & effects.
  2. Under Virtual backgrounds, click + and choose Add image.
  3. Pick the file and select it.

The logo looks reversed to you. Teams and Zoom mirror your own preview. Everyone else on the call sees the logo the right way round.

Good to know

Why it looks the way it does

This is the firm standard, the same for everyone at Aidapt.

  • Live text first

    Your name and details are real text, so they read even when a recipient blocks images. The pictures are small, and every detail, icon and the booking link is something they can tap.

  • System fonts in the inbox

    Brand fonts cannot travel by email. Recipients see Segoe UI on Windows and Helvetica on Mac and iPhone. That is expected.

  • Ready for dark mode

    The logo sits on its own white tile, the icons carry white glyphs and the photo is a clean circle, so all of it stays legible when someone reads in dark mode.

  • If a picture goes missing

    Some editors drop pasted pictures. Paste again with Copy signature. If the logo still drops, delete the empty box, click the picture button in the signature editor and insert the logo file, sized 112 by 36.

  • Keep it as is

    No quotes, banners or badges, and no changes to the fonts or colours. A calm signature is part of how clients read the firm.

  • Title

    Your title is your committed client facing title. If it changes, ask for a new kit rather than editing the signature by hand.
